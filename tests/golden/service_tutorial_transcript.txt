# Welcome to D12 Ball
You are playing **Dinky**, and the next five turns are a guided warm-up. I will tell you what is on the board, what each button does and which card to pick; everything else is greyed out while we go, so you cannot get lost.

## 1. Your first turn
Both sides are dealt the standard **2-2-2** -- two cards in your own zone, two in midfield, two in the zone you are attacking -- and the ball is yours, on **space 4**.

The field is **seven spaces** across in three zones: your own Zone (spaces 1-2), midfield (spaces 3-5), and the Zone you are attacking (spaces 6-7). Your six cards are on the board as meeples and three more wait on your bench. The two numbers on a card are that player's **offensive** and **defensive skill**.

A turn starts with whoever is standing on the ball -- the **handler**. You have one player there, your playmaker, so they are chosen for you. Then you decide what they do.

A handler can do three things: shoot, maneuver, or call a time out to buy both coaches a pause. **You are not offered the shot** -- a shot may only be taken from inside your shooting range, which is space 5 and beyond, and you are one space short of it.

### Choosing a maneuver
A maneuver is a fight for the ball between two players, and both sides pick a card **secretly and at the same time**. Your hand holds three:

- **Low Pass** -- ball to the nearest teammate ahead or behind, up to 2 spaces. Ball speed +1.
- **Dribble Advance** -- your player *and* the ball move forward, and you may change the ball's speed.
- **High Pass** -- the ball flies 2 or more spaces forward.

Which card wins is not a roll. The six form a **cycle**, like rock-paper-scissors with six hands: each beats one of the other side's, ties with one, and loses to one. The Maneuver Reference button draws the whole thing, and you can open it whenever you like.

Dinky's playmaker is standing on the ball, so they are the one challenging you.

**Pick Dribble Advance.** It beats Deflect, which is what Dinky has played, and it is the card that carries the ball up the field.

## 2. Every role has an ability
Dribble Advance beat Deflect outright -- no dice, because the cycle had already settled it -- and your playmaker carried the ball **two** spaces, from space 4 to **space 6**.

Moving forward two spaces is not the ordinary move. A dribble advances typically moves the playerone space. But your **Playmaker** is a skilled dribbler and can move up to two: that is the Playmaker role ability. **Every player has a role and every role has an ability**, printed on their card and worth reading before you pick a maneuver -- the Fullback throws a longer High Pass, the Midfielder gets +3 on a Low Pass or Pressure test, the Defender steals the ball off a won Pressure. Two commands list them whenever you want: **`/d12ball role_abilities_reference`** for all six, and **`/d12ball team_roster`** gives you a list of your team with their roles.

### When both cards are the same rank
Every maneuver has a **rank**, and two cards of the same rank **tie**. A tie is not a draw -- it goes to a **skill test**: both players roll a d12 and add their skill, the attacker their offensive and the defender their defensive, plus whatever their ability is worth. Highest total takes it, and both players pick up an **exhaustion token** for the effort. Exhaustion is the game's running cost; enough of it and a player risks getting injured.

**Pick Low Pass.** It is rank 1, just like the Deflect Dinky has played, so this one goes to the dice -- and Dinky is going to win it. Watch what a won Deflect does: it knocks the ball back a space, nothing more. It only comes **loose** -- open to both sides -- when it lands where nobody is standing. Dinky already has a midfielder right there on space 5, so the ball is simply theirs: no roll, no contest, never loose.

### The other three cards
Defending, your hand is the other half of the cycle:

- **Deflect** -- knocks the ball back a space, nothing more. Land on an empty space and *that's* what makes it loose, open to both sides; land where only one side already is and it's simply theirs, no contest -- you were on the wrong end of that last turn; land where both are and they contest it.
- **Steal** -- takes the ball outright. A turnover.
- **Pressure** -- drives the handler and the ball back a space and moves your challenger forward onto them. Push a handler past their own goal line with it and they have an **own goal** to roll for.

**Pick Pressure.** Dinky is dribbling, and Pressure beats Dribble Advance -- so instead of losing a space you will take one off them.

### High Pass, and what a shot is up against
**Pick High Pass.** Dinky is playing Steal, and High Pass beats it, so the pass gets through.

You will be asked how far to throw. **2 spaces** is the only distance offered here -- a longer throw would run off the end of the field, and the menu never offers one that would. It is also the distance you want: a pass of 2 is caught cleanly, where a 3-space throw has to be *won* by whoever it lands near, with the ball's speed counting against them.

Then take the shot. A score attempt is your d12 plus the shooter's offensive skill and half the ball's speed, against Dinky's d12 plus every defender in the way -- one standing **on** the ball adds all of their defensive skill, one further back adds half, rounded up. Ties go to the shooter.

Their fullback is standing on space 7, so you get their whole defensive skill of 6 against you. Even so: your striker rolls **d12+11** against their **d12+6**. That is a real roll and it can miss -- but you should score about six times in seven.

# That's the tutorial
The rails are off -- every button is live from here, and Dinky is playing for real.

What the warm-up did not cover, the game will: halftime, injuries and recovery, out-of-bounds balls, ceding possession, and the extreme shootout that settles a level score at full time. The first time the game offers you a **Coaching Choice** -- your own window to substitute and rearrange -- I will explain that one too.

Worth knowing:
- `/d12ball rules_search` -- any one section of the rules.
- `/d12ball role_abilities_reference` -- what each role can do.
- `/d12ball maneuver_reference` -- the defeat cycle.
- `/d12ball resume` -- puts the current question back up if a prompt ever goes missing.

Good luck.

# The D12Ball Charter: Laws of the Game -- outline

The section map for the Charter, Law by Law, with the heading in
[docs/living-rules.md](../living-rules.md) each one is made from and
what changes on the way. Read [plan.md](plan.md) first: this is the
shape, that is the reasoning. Numbers here are the ones the built book
will carry, so a Learn to Play citation written against this outline
survives the writing.

**Legend.** *From:* the living-rules heading the Law is edited out of.
*New:* text the living rules do not have. *Moves:* text that leaves the
living-rules heading it is under today.

## Front matter

- **Title page.** *The D12Ball Charter: Laws of the Game.* Edition and
  date. One line: *This document is the final authority on the rules of
  D12 Ball.*
- **How to read this document** (*new*, one page).
  - The numbering: Law, section, paragraph; lettered sub-paragraphs.
    A number in parentheses is a cross-reference.
  - Precedence: the Charter over the Learn to Play, over the text on a
    card or a board, over the bot. Where the Learn to Play simplifies,
    it says so.
  - Basic mode and advanced mode: Laws 1-17 are the whole game in basic
    mode; Part II is what advanced mode adds and changes. A rule in
    Part II wins over a rule in Part I for a game played in advanced
    mode, and nowhere else.
  - Defined terms are bold where defined and listed in Appendix B.
  - *Notes* under a paragraph explain; they are never rules and never
    numbered.
  - The point of view: every rule is written from the side it applies
    to. *Forward* and *back* point opposite ways for the two teams.
- **Contents.** Generated by the builder from the Laws and sections.

## Part I -- The game

### Law 1. The game in brief

*From:* The game in brief.

- 1.1 Two coaches, nine players each, six on the field.
- 1.2 Two periods on one clock, 00-15 and 16-30.
- 1.3 The turn: the team in possession shoots, maneuvers or calls a
  time out; whoever has the ball takes the next turn.
- 1.4 Winning: the higher score at full time; a level score goes to the
  extreme shootout. Every game is settled.

### Law 2. Components and definitions

*From:* Components, and "Words these rules use". *Moves:* every term
the living rules define in passing elsewhere is defined here once.

- 2.1 **The field.** The row of spaces, the three zones, the three
  boards (table), the zone labels per board. Space codes (H1, M2, V3).
- 2.2 **Forward and back.** Home attacks the visitors goal; the two
  words point opposite ways for the two sides. A space belongs to
  nobody; distances count straight across zone boundaries.
- 2.3 **Shooting range.** The far part of the field from the middle;
  the table per board; the middle space is in neither range.
- 2.4 **The kickoff space.** The middle space of midfield, which both
  sides kick off from. Every arrangement covers its own (forward
  reference to 14.6).
- 2.5 **Occupancy and coverage.** Sharing is free in play; coverage
  binds placement, per team; the surplus stacks.
- 2.6 **Players, roles and skills.** Nine players; offense 1-6 and
  defense its inverse; the role table with each ability; the squad's
  composition (1-2-1-2-1-2 by role).
- 2.7 **The ball, the dice and the tokens.** The ball is a d12 and its
  face is its speed; every roll is a d12; the six maneuver cards; tokens
  sit on the card.
- 2.8 **Definitions** (one paragraph each, the bold term first):
  *card* and *meeple*; *handler*; *challenger*; *carrier* (the player a
  resolution leaves the ball with, 4.2); *arrangement*; *set-up*;
  *skill test*; *contest*; *sending a player*; *loose*; *space minute*;
  *Exhausted*; *Injured*; *behind* (advanced, 19.3 -- listed here,
  defined there); *live ball* and *dead ball*.

### Law 3. Setting up a game

*From:* Setting up a game.

- 3.1 **The toss.** Board choice; the coin; the winner picks home or
  visitors; home kicks off the first half, the visitors the second.
- 3.2 **The standard deal.** 2-2-2 by role; the two ends of a goal
  zone; midfield packs from a side's own end; the board 9 deal spelled
  out. *Note:* why midfield packs.
- 3.3 **Setup coaching.** Home then the visitors; unlimited
  substitutions; a player taken off returns to the bench; each finishes
  covering their kickoff space; the positions become the first
  arrangement.
- 3.4 **Kickoff.** Ball on the kickoff space, speed 1, home's, clock 00.

### Law 4. The turn

*From:* The turn.

- 4.1 **The order of a turn.** The six steps, as a numbered list;
  whoever has the ball at the end takes the next turn.
- 4.2 **Choosing the handler.** Any possessing player on the ball's
  space, unless the last resolution left it with one player (the
  carrier). 4.2.2 the list of resolutions that name a carrier; 4.2.3 the
  ones that name nobody; 4.2.4 it holds for one turn; the carrier never
  runs back (forward reference to 12.3).
- 4.3 **Choosing the action.** A shot only in range; a maneuver always;
  a time out only out of range, once a half, and not under last
  possession.
- 4.4 **Finishing the turn.** The ball at rest first; then the
  turnover; then the clock, charged whether or not the ball turned
  over; what costs no time; the last-possession check.

### Law 5. The score attempt

*From:* Score attempt.

- 5.1 **When a shot may be taken.** Only from within range.
- 5.2 **The roll.** One d12 each; what the attack adds; what the
  defense adds (5.3); equal or higher scores.
- 5.3 **What the defense adds.** On the ball, whole; between the ball
  and the goal, half rounded up, per player; behind the ball, nothing.
- 5.4 **Cost.** A shot costs 1 space minute and its shooter nothing;
  a set-up's shot costs a token after the roll, and its minute on top.
- 5.5 **After the shot.** Goal or miss, the ball is dead and a new play
  follows (12.4); a goal restarts from the conceding side's kickoff
  space; a miss restarts on the defending side's closest space, with a
  pickup if nobody is there (10.6).

### Law 6. Maneuvers

*From:* Maneuvers. The six basic cards are 6.5-6.10, one section each,
in the living rules' fixed shape: rank line, effect, role ability,
what the ball is left with.

- 6.1 **The two players.** The handler; a defender on the ball
  challenges and cannot decline; the defending coach may send a player
  (Law 9) or send nobody; an unchallenged maneuver succeeds outright.
- 6.2 **Choosing and revealing.** Secretly and together; three cards a
  side in basic mode. (The gambit sentence moves to 19.2.)
- 6.3 **Who wins.** The cycle table; equal ranks tie; the injured
  participant's two changes (cross-reference to 15.4).
- 6.4 **The skill test.** A token each, then a d12 each; what each side
  adds; the Midfielder's +3; the speed modifier for Steal; a tie is
  rolled again at a token each; injury checks when it resolves. (Double
  Team's second skill and Intercept move to Part II.)
- 6.5 **Low Pass.** Destinations; nearer blocks farther; a different
  player; the passer's step across a shared space; speed +1; the ball
  is left with the receiver; no legal destination; *Winger*.
- 6.6 **Dribble Advance.** One space with the ball; the speed change;
  *Playmaker*.
- 6.7 **High Pass.** 2 or 3, a Fullback's 4; offered distances; the
  three landings (table); a 2 short of range is still received; the
  overshoot; the passer never receives their own pass; out of play with
  nobody to throw to.
- 6.8 **Deflect.** One space back, speed -1, nobody moves; *Fullback*;
  what happens next is Law 10; the overshoot onto a defender is a
  set-up.
- 6.9 **Steal.** Possession flips, speed 1, one space back, the speed
  change; the interceptor is the carrier.
- 6.10 **Pressure.** Handler and ball back one, the challenger onto
  them; no change of possession; against the goal line, the own-goal
  roll (Law 11); *Defender*.

### Law 7. Ball speed

*From:* Ball speed. The gambit amounts move to Part II; the basic Law
lists only the four basic changes.

- 7.1 The speed and its modifier (table).
- 7.2 What changes it in basic mode: Low Pass, Deflect, Dribble
  Advance, Steal.
- 7.3 Every turnover resets it to 1. (The Dribble Burst exception is
  stated at 19.6, not here.)
- 7.4 Where the modifier is added, and nowhere else: the score attempt,
  the defense's Steal, the High Pass contest. Never where the ball
  came to rest.
- 7.5 The overshoot turns the sign.

### Law 8. Scoring opportunities

*From:* Scoring opportunities.

- 8.1 What a set-up is: an ordinary shot out of turn, and the range
  rule still applies.
- 8.2 The four set-ups (table).
- 8.3 The shooter's token; the Striker's +3.
- 8.4 Declining, and the overshoot's exception.
- 8.5 The shot's own minute.

### Law 9. Sending a player

*From:* Sending a player.

- 9.1 The three rules that send: a challenge, a loose ball (the High
  Pass contest included), a pickup.
- 9.2 The two nearest, one each way; ties are the coach's choice.
- 9.3 A player already there is not sent.
- 9.4 The cost: a token a space.
- 9.5 Sending nobody is legal wherever it is offered.
- 9.6 Nothing brings a sent player home.

### Law 10. Contests for the ball

*From:* Contests for the ball.

- 10.1 **Where the ball comes to rest.** The three answers (table);
  "loose" means the space was empty; several players on the space.
- 10.2 **The contest.** Offensive against defensive skill; the last
  possessor's side attacks; a tie at a token each; no modifier.
- 10.3 **A loose ball.** Both send, one sends, neither sends.
- 10.4 **The High Pass contest.** Three or more onto a teammate; the
  one exemption that allows a send against an occupied space; the
  thrower's modifier, against them on an overshoot; a pass that reaches
  nobody carries none.
- 10.5 **Out of bounds.** Neither side sends; the other side's ball; a
  new play, then a pickup.
- 10.6 **Picking the ball up.** The four occasions; a placement, not a
  contest; the usual cost, and free after a time out.
- 10.7 The ball ending up with the side that did not have it is a
  steal.

### Law 11. The own goal

*From:* Own goal.

- 11.1 Only a Pressure that runs out of field.
- 11.2 The roll: two d12, keep the higher, add offense, 7 or more
  avoids; a token either way; not a skill test.
- 11.3 Avoided: the ball stays, the side keeps it, both sides reset, a
  pickup if uncovered.
- 11.4 Conceded: the goal, recorded against the player; the restart.

### Law 12. Turnovers

*From:* Turnovers.

- 12.1 Every turnover resets speed to 1. 12.2 The two kinds (table).
- 12.3 Only a turnover moves anybody.
- 12.4 **Running back after a steal.** Who runs (the two groups); who
  does not (the carrier; a player alone in their zone); where to; the
  cost; a stack breaks up only while a space is uncovered; the coach's
  choice; running back never changes an arrangement.
- 12.5 **Resetting after a new play.** Every meeple back to its
  arrangement, free, before any Coaching Choice; an out-of-bounds ball
  is picked up after.

### Law 13. The time out

*From:* Time out.

- 13.1 What it is: possession stays, the ball stays.
- 13.2 The three conditions.
- 13.3 A side with nobody to bring on may still call one.
- 13.4 The cost: 1 space minute, no tokens.
- 13.5 Both coaches take a Coaching Choice, the caller first.
- 13.6 The free pickup.

### Law 14. The Coaching Choice

*From:* Coaching Choice.

- 14.1 A pause; any number of actions, any order, no cost.
- 14.2 The four actions (table).
- 14.3 The five occasions (table); the shootout's offers the
  substitution alone.
- 14.4 A new play always offers one; passing takes the reply with it.
- 14.5 The substitution budgets: two a half across every window; two
  more at halftime; one before the shootout; seven in a game.
- 14.6 Who may come on: the bench first, then healthy back-bench
  players; never an injured player; half the tokens back.
- 14.7 Formations (table); read from a coach's own goal; the two
  board-9 shapes; the re-deal and the stack space; positioning after.
- 14.8 Finishing: the kickoff space must be covered; the positions
  become the arrangement; passing keeps the old one.

### Law 15. Exhaustion and injury

*From:* Exhaustion and injury. The Cyborg sentences move to 20.3.

- 15.1 Gaining tokens (table); nothing else costs; an injured player
  gains none.
- 15.2 Becoming Exhausted: tokens exceed defensive skill.
- 15.3 The injury check: when; higher than the token count is safe;
  the test's own tokens count; only a skill test causes one.
- 15.4 Playing injured: tokens lost, none gained; the maneuver
  disadvantage against a healthy opponent; the contest disadvantage;
  what is not withheld; the two escapes; nobody is compelled to
  substitute.
- 15.5 Recovery: halftime's token and the coach's pick; the back
  bench's half.

### Law 16. The clock

*From:* The clock.

- 16.1 One clock, two periods, never stops.
- 16.2 Space minutes (table); a maneuver's cost is flat.
- 16.3 Last possession: the trigger; the action finishes in full;
  whoever holds the ball; their next turnover ends the period with
  nothing after it; the clock runs through it.
- 16.4 Halftime: recovery; the visitors' Coaching Choice then home's,
  two substitutions each and counted against neither half; the second
  half starts at 16; a goal is recorded with its period.
- 16.5 Full time: the first turnover under last possession; the higher
  score; a level score to the shootout after one substitution each.

### Law 17. The extreme shootout

*From:* Extreme shootout.

- 17.1 Every level game is settled here.
- 17.2 The six on the field after the full-time substitution; nothing
  recovers.
- 17.3 The secret order; the top card revealed together.
- 17.4 Each test: d12 plus offense, no defender; an injured shooter
  rolls bare; a tie scores for neither; no tokens, no injury check.
- 17.5 A shootout goal is a goal.
- 17.6 A round of six; stopping early; sudden death; a new round.

## Part II -- Advanced mode

### Law 18. Advanced mode

*New*, from the opening paragraph of the living rules and the first
paragraph of Gambits.

- 18.1 Advanced mode turns on two modules, the gambits (Law 19) and the
  species abilities (Law 20). A game may take one, the other or both,
  declared before the toss.
- 18.2 A rule in Part II wins over Part I in a game playing the module
  it belongs to; in basic mode Part II does not exist.
- 18.3 The exceptions Part II makes to Part I, in one table, each with
  both numbers: the Dribble Burst's speed (7.3 / 19.6), the Cyborg's
  threshold (15.2 / 20.3), Damaged for Injured (15.4 / 20.3), Mind
  Pull's and Overdrive's tokens (15.1 / 20.4, 20.3), the second
  challenger's skill (6.4 / 19.10), Intercept's modifier (6.4 / 19.9).

### Law 19. Gambits

*From:* Gambits.

- 19.1 A gambit is the advanced version of the basic maneuver on its
  rank (table); rank alone decides; the cycle is unchanged.
- 19.2 A gambit is played only against a challenge; six cards, not
  three.
- 19.3 **Who may play a gambit.** *Behind*: fewer goals, or more
  Exhausted-or-Injured players on the field; read when the cards are
  chosen; public; both may hold them.
- 19.4 **When a gambit's effect fires.** The cards decide (table:
  won, lost, tied); the three consequences, each its own lettered
  sub-paragraph.
- 19.5 Skilled Pass. 19.6 Dribble Burst. 19.7 Setup Pass. 19.8 Clear.
  19.9 Intercept. 19.10 Double Team. Each in the six-part shape: rank
  line; effect; role ability; what the ball is left with; *beaten*;
  what it changes in Part I (with the number).

### Law 20. Species abilities

*From:* Species abilities.

- 20.1 One ability per species; every player of the species has it on
  either side; in basic mode species is only a name.
- 20.2 **Volatile (Fire Demon).** The ignite; blaze and burn; every
  roll it covers; the four skill-test cases, lettered; outside a skill
  test it is only the number.
- 20.3 **Lithium Powered (Cyborg).** Drain; Drained at 7; Damaged;
  Overdrive; Charge-up, and what "moves" means; a new-play reset is
  not a run back.
- 20.4 **Mind Pull (Telekinetic).** The trigger; the roll; whoever the
  movement moved is not offered it; one roll per Telekinetic per
  movement; only a live ball; it resolves before the ball settles and
  is a steal. **Smooth**: the other half; who is not offered it; not a
  turnover; the one movement it cannot pre-empt; Smooth is asked first.
- 20.5 **Slimey (Ooze).** Merge in a skill test and a contest; Merge in
  a score attempt; Spreadable.
